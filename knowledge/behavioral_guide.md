# Behavioral Interview Guide

## Topic: Dealing with Failure
When answering a question about dealing with failure, use the STAR method. 
First, outline the Situation and Task. Then detail the Action you took that resulted in failure, taking full ownership and avoiding blaming others. 
Most importantly, explain the Result and what you learned from the experience. Highlight how you applied that learning to a future successful project.

## Topic: Team Conflict
To answer questions about team conflict, focus on communication and empathy. 
Describe a scenario where you had a disagreement with a coworker. Explain how you initiated a private, 1-on-1 conversation to understand their perspective. 
Show how you compromised or found a mutually beneficial solution that prioritized the project's goals over personal ego.
